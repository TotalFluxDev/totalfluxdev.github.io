Privacy Policy

Last updated August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TotalFlux ("TotalFlux," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with TimeBox, our macOS productivity application ("TimeBox" or the "App"), and the TotalFlux website and support services that link to this Privacy Policy.

TimeBox is designed to operate locally on your Mac. We do not operate user accounts for TimeBox, and the App does not require you to provide personal information in order to use its core functionality.

TimeBox is designed with privacy in mind: we do not collect or transmit personal information from the App for our own analytics, advertising, profiling, or other purposes. The App does not independently send your timer activity or other application data to TotalFlux.

1. Information We Collect

Information collected by TimeBox

TimeBox does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal information to use the App's core features.

TimeBox is designed to keep its application data on your Mac rather than transmitting that data to TotalFlux. We do not intentionally collect or maintain a centralized database of TimeBox users or their timer activity.

TimeBox does not collect or transmit personal information to TotalFlux for analytics, advertising, behavioral profiling, or similar purposes.

Information you provide to us

If you contact TotalFlux for support, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, macOS version, TimeBox version, and information about a problem you are experiencing. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support.

Website information

The TotalFlux and TimeBox websites are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not intentionally collect personal information through forms, accounts, chat widgets, analytics services, or advertising trackers on these websites.

As with any website, the hosting provider may process technical information necessary to deliver web pages, maintain the service, and protect its infrastructure. We do not control that infrastructure data and do not use it to build user profiles or track your activity across websites.

2. Apple Services and App Store Analytics

TimeBox is distributed through Apple's Mac App Store. Apple may provide TotalFlux with aggregated and privacy-protected information about TimeBox through App Store Connect, such as downloads, sales, usage trends, crashes, and other app performance metrics.

This information is provided and processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices and is subject to Apple's privacy protections and applicable eligibility thresholds. TimeBox does not independently collect or transmit personal information to TotalFlux for analytics purposes.

Apple may also process information in connection with the App Store, software distribution, payments, crash reporting, and other Apple services. Those activities are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

3. How We Use Information

When you contact us, we use the information you provide to communicate with you, investigate reported problems, provide customer support, and improve TimeBox.

We may also use information when reasonably necessary to protect the security of our services, prevent abuse, comply with applicable law, or respond to lawful requests from government authorities.

TimeBox's core functionality does not depend on collecting personal information from you, and TotalFlux does not use personal information from the App for advertising or behavioral profiling.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

If you contact us for support, information contained in your support communication may necessarily be processed by the email service providers involved in sending and receiving that email.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce our rights, protect the security of our services or users, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data Retention

Because TimeBox does not require an account or transmit timer activity to TotalFlux, we do not maintain a server-side history of your TimeBox usage.

If you contact us by email, we may retain your correspondence and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide support, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

TimeBox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the App or our websites. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at totalflux-help@yahoo.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information that we hold about you, including rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain processing of your personal information. You may also have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Because we generally do not collect personal information through TimeBox itself, there may be little or no personal information held by TotalFlux in connection with your use of the App. If you have a privacy request, contact us at totalflux-help@yahoo.com.

8. Tracking and Do-Not-Track

TimeBox does not use advertising trackers or cross-app behavioral tracking. The TotalFlux and TimeBox websites do not intentionally use analytics or advertising tracking technologies.

Our websites are hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information as part of providing and securing its hosting service. We do not control or use GitHub's infrastructure data for our own advertising or profiling.

Because we do not intentionally use tracking technologies on our websites, there is currently no separate TotalFlux tracking system for which a browser-based Do-Not-Track preference needs to be honored.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TimeBox, our services, or applicable legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
