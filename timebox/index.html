TimeBox

Focused work, without the distractions.

TimeBox is a simple Pomodoro timer for macOS that keeps your focus sessions close at hand, helping you work with intention without getting in your way.

Support

Make time work for you.

Breaking work into focused sessions creates a simple boundary around your attention. TimeBox makes those sessions easy to start, easy to see, and easy to finish so you can spend less time managing your workflow and more time doing the work.

Built to protect your attention.

Every part of TimeBox is designed to remove friction between deciding to focus and actually starting.

Stay focused.

Losing awareness of time usually means breaking concentration to check another app.

TimeBox lives quietly in the macOS menu bar, so your current session stays visible without occupying valuable screen space or pulling you out of context.

Less to manage.

Many productivity tools ask you to configure systems before they become useful.

TimeBox keeps the experience intentionally simple, so you can begin a focused session without setting up workflows, projects, accounts, or dashboards.

Your timer, your pace.

Not every task fits the same rhythm, and rigid defaults can work against your process.

TimeBox lets you choose the session and break durations that work for you, adapting to your preferred workflow instead of forcing a fixed schedule.

Know when to stop.

Watching a clock interrupts deep work, but missing the end of a session can erase the value of a break.

Notifications and alarms give you a clear boundary between focused work and rest, so you can stay present without constantly checking the timer.

Designed for macOS.

Generic tools can feel disconnected from how your desktop already works.

TimeBox is built around the menu bar and familiar macOS interaction patterns, so it feels like part of your system rather than another layer on top of it.

Your focus stays yours.

TimeBox works locally on your Mac. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud services, or tracking.

You can use TimeBox without creating an account or handing your usage data to another service.
